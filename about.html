About me

Curiosity grows through experience.

Hello! I am Harish Divakar, a senior at Troy Athens High School in Troy, Michigan. I am interested in applied mathematics, data science, and music.

My story

From Chennai to Michigan

I was raised in Chennai, a city in southern India, and moved to Michigan in 2019. Moving to the United States introduced me to a more hands-on style of learning alongside traditional academics.

One experience that stayed with me was building paper roller coasters while learning kinetic energy. Applying calculations to real constraints showed me that learning could extend beyond the page and helped me discover new interests.

I now apply myself inside and outside school through computer and math clubs, marching band as a section leader, jazz band, personal projects, and my work as a courtesy clerk at Kroger.
